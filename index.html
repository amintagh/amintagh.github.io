Born in Tehran, Iran, in 1947, Ali Taghipour studied at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) the School of Planning and Architecture in New Delhi. He later completed a Master’s degree in Architecture at the University of Tehran and earned a PhD from the University of Paris 7 (Jussieu), France. His doctoral research examined the impact of industrialization on urban design in developing countries, using Iran as a case study.

He served as a Professor of Architecture and Urban Design at the University of Tabriz and worked as an Architect (chef de projet) on several national-level architectural projects, including award-winning competition entries. He was also advisor to the Municipality of Tehran and karaj and Shahriar on urbanism and architecture and served as a consultant for cultural and urban complexes across Tehran, Tabriz, and Karaj.

Dividing his life between Iran and France, he began his artistic practice in painting and sculpture in 1980. Following several solo and group exhibitions in Paris and Tehran—including at the Lazarian Art Gallery—sculpting and Painting became his primary focus.
