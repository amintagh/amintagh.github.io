Biography

Born in Tehran, Iran, in 1947, he studied at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) and the School of Planning and Architecture in New Delhi. He later completed a Master’s degree in Architecture at the University of Tehran and earned a PhD from University of Paris 7 (Jussieu)- France where his doctoral research examined the impact of industrialization on urban design in developing countries, using Iran as a case study.He served as a Professor of Architecture and Urban Design at the University of Tabriz and worked as a lead project manager (chef de projet) on several national-level architectural projects, including award-winning competition entries. He also acted as an urbanism and architecture advisor to the Municipality of Tehran and served as a consulting engineer for numerous cultural and urban complexes across Tehran, Tabriz, and Karaj.Dividing his life between Iran and France, he began his artistic practice in painting and sculpture in 1990. Following several solo and group exhibitions in Paris and Tehran, sculpture has become his primary focus.
